Econometric Insights & Strategic Implications

1. Resolving the Omitted Variable Bias

In simple regression (Model 1), governance was non-significant (p = .244) because large-cap firms naturally carry higher market valuations. Once company scale (ln Market Cap and ln Revenue) and sector cost baselines are controlled for, the true negative effect of governance risk emerges unambiguously (p = .037*).

2. The +5.31% Governance Opportunity

Moving from the poorest ISS decile (score 10) to the highest compliance decile (score 1) captures an estimated +5.31% net margin advantage. Governance is an operational performance driver, not a cost center.

3. Diagnostic Robustness

Breusch-Pagan test indicates mild heteroscedasticity (LM = 15.14, p = .004). Robust standard errors (White HC3) confirm governance significance remains robust (p = .041).
