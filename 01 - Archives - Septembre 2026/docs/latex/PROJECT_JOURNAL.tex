\subsection{1. Executive Summary \& Research
Framework}\label{executive-summary-research-framework}

\subsubsection{1.1 The Managerial Problematic (Research
Question)}\label{the-managerial-problematic-research-question}

Corporate executives, board directors, and ESG fund managers face an
ongoing debate: \emph{Does dedicating significant capital and
operational focus to institutional corporate governance and social
responsibility enhance shareholder value, or does it impose costly
bureaucratic frictions that compromise financial profitability?}

To resolve this question empirically, this project establishes a 4-week
analytical trajectory centered on:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{``Does corporate governance risk (ISS QualityScore) compromise
financial profitability and market valuation among S\&P 500 firms, or
does strong governance provide a resilience and performance premium?''}
\end{quote}

\subsubsection{1.2 The 4-Step Answering
Protocol}\label{the-4-step-answering-protocol}

In accordance with course guidelines (Slide 9 \& Slide 41), every
hypothesis and statistical result follows a strict four-step protocol:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{IDENTIFY}: Justify the statistical test exclusively from the
  measurement scale of the variables (Nominal, Ordinal, Continuous).
\item
  \textbf{HYPOTHESISE}: Formulate the Null (\(H_0\)) and Alternative
  (\(H_1\)) hypotheses in plain business language.
\item
  \textbf{DECIDE}: Evaluate the significance level (\(\alpha = 0.05\),
  report exact \(p\)-value or \(p < .001\)) and assess the
  magnitude/effect size (\(r\), \(\eta^2\), Cramér's \(V\), \(R^2\)).
\item
  \textbf{RECOMMEND}: Provide concrete operational directives answering:
  \emph{``What does the manager do on Monday morning?''}
\end{enumerate}

  \end{itemize}

\subsubsection{3.2 Why Full Fiscal Year 2025
(FY2025)?}\label{why-full-fiscal-year-2025-fy2025}

In empirical quantitative finance, using partial current-year figures
(e.g.~Q1/Q2 2026) creates severe seasonal distortion, annualized
extrapolation errors, and interim accounting noise.

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Audited Completeness}: FY2025 provides complete, 12-month
  audited financial statements filed with the U.S. Securities and
  Exchange Commission (SEC).
\item
  \textbf{Macroeconomic Stability}: Captures the full annual economic
  cycle, ensuring valid comparability across sectors with contrasting
  seasonality (e.g., Retail peak in Q4 vs.~Energy fluctuations).
\item
  \textbf{Synchronicity}: Financial ratios for FY2025 align with Q3/Q4
  2025 ISS governance evaluations, ensuring cross-sectional validity.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{4. Comprehensive Data Cleaning \& Remediation
Audit}\label{comprehensive-data-cleaning-remediation-audit}

\subsubsection{\texorpdfstring{4.1 Remediation of Ticker Anomalies:
Fiserv (\texttt{FISV} \(\rightarrow\)
\texttt{FI})}{4.1 Remediation of Ticker Anomalies: Fiserv (FISV \textbackslash rightarrow FI)}}\label{remediation-of-ticker-anomalies-fiserv-fisv-rightarrow-fi}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Diagnosis}: In the raw extract, row 199 (\texttt{FISV})
  exhibited missing values across sector, revenue, profit margin, beta,
  headcount, and governance risk.
\item
  \textbf{Root Cause Analysis}: In 2023, Fiserv, Inc.~transferred its
  listing from NASDAQ to the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) and
  rebranded its ticker symbol from \texttt{FISV} to \texttt{FI}. The raw
  extraction script relied on the legacy ticker symbol, causing Yahoo
  Finance API calls to return null objects.
\item
  \textbf{Remediation}: The pipeline programmatically maps \texttt{FISV}
  to \texttt{FI}, populating its proper sector (\emph{Financial
  Services} \(\rightarrow\) \emph{Finance}), FY2025 revenue (\$19.09B),
  profit margin (16.0\%), beta (0.88), headcount (42,000), and ISS
  governance risk scores.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{\texorpdfstring{4.2 The Case of Negative Book Equity \&
Undefined ROE
(\(N = 32\))}{4.2 The Case of Negative Book Equity \& Undefined ROE (N = 32)}}\label{the-case-of-negative-book-equity-undefined-roe-n-32}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Diagnosis}: 32 major blue-chip corporations exhibited missing
  values (\texttt{NaN}) in the \texttt{ROE} column.

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    Affected firms include: AbbVie (\texttt{ABBV}), Altria Group
    (\texttt{MO}), AutoZone (\texttt{AZO}), Booking Holdings
    (\texttt{BKNG}), Cardinal Health (\texttt{CAH}), Domino's Pizza
    (\texttt{DPZ}), Fair Isaac (\texttt{FICO}), HCA Healthcare
    (\texttt{HCA}), Hilton Worldwide (\texttt{HLT}), HP
    Inc.~(\texttt{HPQ}), and Philip Morris.
  \end{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Root Cause (Corporate Finance Theory)}:
  \[\text{ROE} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Shareholders' Equity}}\]
  Over the past decade, these mature corporations executed aggressive,
  debt-financed share repurchase programs (\emph{treasury stock
  buybacks}). Cumulative buybacks subtracted from retained earnings have
  driven total book value of equity into negative territory. When book
  equity is negative, computing a standard ROE yields either a
  mathematically positive number for loss-making firms or a negative
  ratio for highly profitable firms---rendering ROE economically
  meaningless. Consequently, standard financial databases (including
  Yahoo Finance and Bloomberg) suppress ROE for negative-equity firms.
\item
  \textbf{Remediation \& Pedagogical Strategy}:

  \begin{enumerate}
  \def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
  \tightlist
  \item
    We do \textbf{not} arbitrarily impute or drop these corporations;
    doing so would delete 32 of America's most prominent firms and
    introduce severe selection bias.
  \item
    We retain \texttt{ROE} for the 471 firms where equity is positive
    (\(N = 471\)), while reporting missingness transparently.
  \item
    We introduce \textbf{Net Profit Margin}
    (\(\frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Revenue}}\)) as our co-primary
    profitability metric. Because revenue is always positive for
    operating firms, \texttt{Profit\_Margin} is 100\% defined for all
    \(N = 503\) firms.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{\texorpdfstring{4.3 Extreme Outlier Treatment: Masco
Corporation
(\texttt{MAS})}{4.3 Extreme Outlier Treatment: Masco Corporation (MAS)}}\label{extreme-outlier-treatment-masco-corporation-mas}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Diagnosis}: Masco Corp displayed an extreme ROE of \(58.625\)
  (\(5,862.5\%\)).
\item
  \textbf{Root Cause}: Masco held a tiny positive equity balance
  (\(\approx \$80\text{M}\)) against nearly \$1B in net profits, causing
  the ratio denominator to approach zero.
\item
  \textbf{Remediation}: In accordance with course guidelines (Slide 21:
  \emph{``The mean is destroyed by outliers. The median is robust to
  outliers and skew''}), we preserve the authentic observation without
  artificial truncation or winsorization. We explicitly report both the
  mean (\(\bar{x} = 39.5\%\)) and median (\(\text{Mdn} = 16.8\%\)) and
  use the median as the true managerial measure of central tendency.
\end{itemize}
